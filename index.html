How the Power Score is Calculated

Each metric is first converted to a 0–100 scale relative to the rest of the field, so no single stat's raw magnitude dominates. The weighted components are then summed into a final score.

🏆
Championships
Titles won vs. most titles held by any single owner
📊
Adjusted OPR
Career average Adj. OPR vs. highest in the league — cross-era comparable
⚡
Win %

🎯
Points Scored
Average points per season vs. highest in the league
🏟
Playoff Rate
Playoff appearances ÷ seasons played, scaled to 0–100
⏳
Tenure Bonus
+5 pts for playing 75%+ of all seasons · +2 pts for 50–74% — rewards loyalty through rebuilds
Min. 3 seasons to qualify  ·  Scores are relative to the current field and will shift as the league grows
